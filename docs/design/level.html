Bullseye and horizon

One app, two views. Lying flat it is the round bubble vial (design A); stood on an edge it switches to the attitude horizon (design B), which now shows both angles: the rotation (level or plumb) and the up / down lean, as horizon tilt and pitch ladder. Both screens below are live at 368 × 448 and follow the same simulated sensor. Drag on a screen to tilt it, or use the panel.

Tilt front / back

The flat sliders drive the left screen, the edge sliders the right one. Green means level (within 0.3°), yellow means close (within 2°). On the device the accelerometer is read at about 100 Hz and smoothed, and the gyroscope steadies it while the board moves, so the bubble glides instead of jittering. A short beep can confirm when it reaches level, and Zero is remembered across sleeps.
