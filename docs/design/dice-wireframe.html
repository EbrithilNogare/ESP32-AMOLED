No roll yet. Try a d20 with advantage, or 6d6.

Advantage throws the whole set twice and keeps the higher total; disadvantage keeps the lower. On a single d20 that is the standard 5e rule. d100 is thrown as percentile dice, a tens d10 (00–90) and a ones d10 (0–9), with 00 + 0 counting as 100. Up to 10 dice per throw (4 for d100). On the device it can also roll on a shake, using the board’s QMI8658 motion sensor.
