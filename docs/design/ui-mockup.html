Every screen of the current firmware, clickable, at the panel's real pixel size with its tokens, fonts and icons: the nine apps, the quick settings you pull down from the menu's status bar, and the Wi-Fi setup with its phone keypad. The side buttons behave like a phone's: Power turns the screen off and on (hold it to power off), the other one is Back (press) and Home (hold). The idle timeline runs for real.

  • Tap an icon to open an app. Scroll the menu with the wheel or trackpad.
  • Pull the status bar down (or tap it) for quick settings: slide the brightness or volume bar (sliding it unmutes), tap the speaker to mute, tap Wi-Fi to pick a network and type its password with multi-tap (2 2 2 = c, # = abc/ABC/123). In this mock any 8+ character password connects; "wrongpass" shows the failure. The radio runs only while something needs it, so the row usually reads "Off"; switch its state under Wi-Fi.
  • Dice: tap the middle to roll. Level: drag on the screen to tilt the board; switch flat / edge under Sensor.
  • Power (upper side button, BOOT on the board, or P): press to turn the screen off (it collapses to a line, then a dot), press again to turn it back on in the same app. With the screen off, touch does nothing.
  • Hold Power for 1.5 s: a red ring winds round the power symbol and the device powers off; Power then starts it again, in the menu.
  • Back (lower side button, PWR on the board, or Esc): one step back. Hold it for 1 s (or H) for Home: the menu.
  • On battery the screen dims after 30 s and goes off after 40 s (Bot face, Espresso, Level and Wi-Fi setup stay bright 60 s). While dimmed, the first tap or press only wakes the screen.
  • Bot face: it wanders slowly round the screen and now and then winks, glances aside, hums (two hops) or yawns (mostly when dimmed). Tap it for a smile, twice quickly for surprise.
  • System → Touch: tap the five crosses, then drag to check the dot follows; Reset to factory drops the calibration.
  • Start the Pomodoro and leave it alone: after 30 s it switches to its minimal view instead of going off.
  • Timer: the Clock tab never blanks: after 30 s it drops to a minimal clock at 25 % that moves every minute. A running stopwatch or countdown does the same, even from the menu. Set the countdown by dragging a column up or down, or tapping above / below its middle; at 0 it turns the screen on, flashes and beeps (Back or Power silences it).
  • Noise: tap to play or pause, swipe sideways for rain, white or blue noise (it really plays, at the quick-settings volume). Playing, the screen dims and goes off as usual and the sound goes on, also from the menu or after a Power press; powering off stops it.

A mock of the firmware, not the firmware: colours, sizes and fonts follow docs/UI_DESIGN.md, but the real panel is far brighter and sharper than a monitor, so brightness is shown compressed here. Values like IP, battery, networks and firmware version are examples.
