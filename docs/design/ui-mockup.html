  • Tap an icon to open an app. Scroll the menu with the wheel or trackpad.
  • Pull the status bar down (or tap it) for quick settings: slide the brightness bar, tap Wi-Fi to pick a network and type its password with multi-tap (2 2 2 = c, # = abc/ABC/123). In this mock any 8+ character password connects; "wrongpass" shows the failure.
  • Dice: tap the middle to roll. Level: drag on the screen to tilt the board; switch flat / edge under Sensor.
  • Back (upper side button, or Esc): one step back. Home (lower, or H): menu.
  • Hold either button for 1 s: power-off animation, then deep sleep. Press again to wake.
  • While dimmed or black, the first tap only wakes the screen.
  • Start the Pomodoro and leave it alone: after 10 s it switches to its minimal view instead of sleeping.

A mock of the firmware, not the firmware: colours, sizes and fonts follow docs/UI_DESIGN.md, but the real panel is far brighter and sharper than a monitor, so brightness is shown compressed here. Values like IP, battery, networks and firmware version are examples.
