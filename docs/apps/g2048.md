# 2048

## Purpose

The classic sliding-tile game: swipe to slide every tile, equal tiles that
collide merge, and the goal is a 2048 tile. A game can go on past it.

- **Score bar:**
  - Two chips: score (with a "+N" that floats up on a merge) and best.
  - A round "new game" button. While a game is running, it asks first: tap
    again within 3 s to confirm.
- **Board:** 344 × 344 px on a `surface` panel with 16 px corners. 4 × 4
  tiles of 76 px with 8 px gaps. Empty cells are `surfaceHi`.
- **Tiles:**
  - 2 and 4 are dark greys with light text.
  - 8 and up run the classic warm palette (orange → red → gold) with dark
    text.
  - Past 2048, tiles go near-black with gold text.
  - Numbers use Roboto Mono: 1–2 digits at 44 px, 3 digits at 32 px, 4–5
    digits at 24 px.
- **Overlays** (an 80 % black scrim over the board):
  - "2048" in large gold digits, with Keep going (once per game).
  - "Game over" with New game.

## Interaction

| Input | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Swipe on the board (≥ 30 px, dominant axis) | slide all tiles that way; if anything moved, a new tile (2 at 90 %, 4 at 10 %) appears |
| New game (tap twice mid-game) | fresh board with two tiles; the best score stays |
| Keep going | closes the win overlay; play on |
| Back | menu (the game is kept) |

**Animation:**
- Tiles slide to their place over 90 ms (ease out).
- A merged tile pops (100 % → 112 % → 100 %, 120 ms).
- The new tile grows in from 0 over 120 ms.
- A swipe during an animation finishes the animation at once and then
  plays.

## Rules (`lib/Game2048`, pure and tested)

- Each row (or column) slides toward the swipe. Equal neighbours merge
  once per move, the pair nearest the wall first: `2 2 2 2 ←` gives
  `4 4 . .`, and `2 2 4 ←` gives `4 4 . .`, not `8`.
- Score: the sum of the merged tiles' values.
- Won the first time a tile reaches 2048. Over when no empty cell and no
  equal neighbours remain.
- Random numbers from the hardware RNG (`esp_random()`); the tests use a
  fixed seed.

## Power

- **Normal:** dims after 30 s and turns off at 40 s on battery. Thinking
  time counts as idle, but the game waits.
- **Screen off:** nothing, so the device deep-sleeps.

## Persistence

| What | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Board, score, won flag, best score | parked (RTC) on every sleep; NVS `g2048` when the app closes and at a power-off |

A wake comes back to the same board. A reset comes back to the board as it
was when the app was last closed or the device last powered off. NVS is
never written at every sleep (ARCHITECTURE §8.5).

```c
#define G2048_COLOR 0xEDC22E  // menu accent: the 2048 tile's gold

```

## Errors

None (no hardware besides touch and the screen).

## Acceptance (on device)

- [ ] Swipes in all four directions slide and merge as in the original game.
- [ ] A swipe that moves nothing adds no tile.
- [ ] Animations feel snappy; fast swipes are never lost.
- [ ] Back, open again: the same board. Sleep and wake: the same board.
- [ ] Best score survives a reset.
